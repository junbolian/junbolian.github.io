My research focuses on data-driven retail pricing, assortment optimization, and large language models for building reliable optimization models. Much of this work is developed with, and tested at, Wenzhou Buyi Pharmacy Chain, where I serve as Chief Analytics Officer; a store-level repricing field experiment there is planned for 2027. For long-tail assortment decisions, I built an agent that places every SKU in a new five-level category system, down to groups of directly substitutable products, scores delisting candidates on sales, margin, price, and trend, and applies protection rules for chronic-disease drugs, assortment coverage, and online best-sellers. Staff accept or reject each recommendation with a reason, and the agent turns these reasons into revised rules for the next round. It has run in production for over six months, with more than 95% of its recommendations accepted, and has cut the company's quarterly delisting review from over 100 hours to under one hour. Earlier work in deep learning and evolutionary computation is listed on [Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/citations?hl=zh-CN&user=swfyyjkAAAAJ).
