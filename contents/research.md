My research centers on operations research and operations management, with a focus on retail pricing, inventory management, and large language models for operations research. Much of the pricing work is developed with, and tested at, Wenzhou Buyi Pharmacy Chain, where I serve as Chief Analytics Officer. Earlier, I worked on deep learning, evolutionary computation, and AI applications in food and agriculture engineering.

#### Operations Research & Operation Management
- **Retail pricing.** How price competition and demand learning work when retailers see only part of their rivals' prices ([Working Paper](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=7620880)), and how to reprice a large assortment without estimated demand curves, with a store-level field experiment scheduled for 2027.
- **Inventory management.** Reliable execution and reusable certification of dual-sourcing inventory policies ([Working Paper](https://dx.doi.org/10.2139/ssrn.7446638)).
- **Large language models for operations research.** Structured modeling and behavioral verification for reliable LLM-based optimization ([ReLoop](https://neurips.cc/virtual/2026/loc/sydney/poster/153462), **NeurIPS 2026**), and label-free certification of LLM-generated optimization models ([AdmitOR](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.15565)).
- **Transportation.** The value of traffic-signal phase information for route choice ([Working Paper](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=7368578)).

#### Deep Learning
Architectures for non-spatial and texture-based recognition. Designed [Twisted Convolutional Networks (TCNs)](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neunet.2025.108451) for non-spatial data classification, and further developed [TwistNet-2D](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.07262) for texture image classification, surpassing the latest state-of-the-art (SOTA) models.

#### Evolutionary Computation
Evolutionary computation theory and large-scale optimization. Invented multiple algorithms including Human Evolutionary Optimization Algorithm (HEOA), Parrot Optimizer (PO), Educational Competition Optimizer (ECO), and Operator-Programmed Algorithms for Landscape-Aware Black-Box Optimization (OPAL), with papers recognized as **ESI Hot Papers** (TOP 0.1%) and **ESI Highly Cited Papers** (TOP 1%).

#### AI in Food & Agriculture Engineering
Interdisciplinary applications of AI in quality prediction and pattern recognition, including mold detection systems for Chinese herbal medicine and spectroscopy-based food inspection.
