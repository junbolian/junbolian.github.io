My research focuses on data-driven retail pricing, assortment optimization, inventory management, and large language models for building reliable optimization models. Much of this work is developed with, and tested at, Wenzhou Buyi Pharmacy Chain, where I serve as Chief Analytics Officer; a store-level repricing field experiment there is planned for 2027. For long-tail assortment decisions, I built an agent that scores SKU potential within a new five-level category system, recommends delisting through rule-based screening with protection rules, and learns from the reasons staff give when they reject a recommendation, revising its scoring and rules to improve the next round of recommendations. It has run in production for over six months, with more than 95% of its recommendations accepted, and has cut the company's quarterly delisting review from over 100 hours to under one hour. Earlier work in deep learning and evolutionary computation is listed on [Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/citations?hl=zh-CN&user=swfyyjkAAAAJ).
