**Junbo Lian** is a Ph.D. student at the [Institute of Operations Research and Analytics (IORA)](https://iora.nus.edu.sg/), [National University of Singapore (NUS)](https://nus.edu.sg/), advised by [Prof. Chung-Piaw Teo](https://iora.nus.edu.sg/people-p/teo-chung-piaw/) and [Prof. Hanzhang Qin](https://hanzhangqin.com/). He is also completing an M.S. in [Machine Learning and Data Science (MLDS)](https://www.mccormick.northwestern.edu/machine-learning-data-science/) at [Northwestern University](https://www.northwestern.edu/). His research focuses on retail pricing, inventory management, and large language models for operations research ([ReLoop](https://neurips.cc/virtual/2026/loc/sydney/poster/153462), **NeurIPS 2026**). He is Chief Analytics Officer at Wenzhou Buyi Pharmacy Chain, a national TOP-100 pharmacy retailer, where he built the company's pricing system and will test his pricing research in a store-level field experiment. Earlier, he worked on evolutionary computation and deep learning, with two *ESI Hot Papers* and four *ESI Highly Cited Papers* (see [Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/citations?hl=zh-CN&user=swfyyjkAAAAJ)). He received the *National Scholarship* in 2024.
