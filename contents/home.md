**Junbo Lian** is a Ph.D. student at the [Institute of Operations Research and Analytics (IORA)](https://iora.nus.edu.sg/), [National University of Singapore (NUS)](https://nus.edu.sg/), advised by [Prof. Chung-Piaw Teo](https://iora.nus.edu.sg/people-p/teo-chung-piaw/) and [Prof. Hanzhang Qin](https://hanzhangqin.com/). He received his M.S. in [Machine Learning and Data Science (MLDS)](https://www.mccormick.northwestern.edu/machine-learning-data-science/) from [Northwestern University](https://www.northwestern.edu/). His research focuses on retail pricing, inventory management, and large language models for operations research. He is Chief Analytics Officer at Wenzhou Buyi Pharmacy Chain, a national TOP-100 pharmacy retailer, where he built the company's pricing system and will test his pricing research in a store-level field experiment. Earlier, he worked on evolutionary computation and deep learning, with two *ESI Hot Papers* and four *ESI Highly Cited Papers*. He received the *National Scholarship* in 2024.
